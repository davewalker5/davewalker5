The repositories you'll find here tend to begin with something I want to **observe, record, model, analyse or understand** — whether that's wildlife, aircraft, fossils, shell growth, stromatolites, sport, music, retrocomputing or something else that has caught my interest.

🔭 At the moment I'm particularly interested in computational natural history, observational data and retrocomputing.

🌱 I'm exploring how small computational models can help explain natural processes and emergent behaviour.

💬 Ask me about RC2014/Z80 programming, aircraft observation, natural-history data, computational modelling, or turning an interesting question into a small software project.

⚡ Fun fact: the subjects here range from stromatolites and cephalopods to aircraft, rugby and 8-bit computers — but most of them ultimately start with the same question:

> What can I make out of it?

The subject matter is fairly eclectic, but much of what you'll find here falls into a few broad themes.

## 🔭 Observation & Recording

I enjoy collecting observations and turning them into useful, queryable datasets.

- [ADS-B BaseStation Reader](https://github.com/davewalker5/ADS-B-BaseStationReader) — local-first aircraft observation and analysis workspace
- [NatureRecorderPy](https://github.com/davewalker5/NatureRecorderPy) — wildlife sightings recorder
- [FossilTracker](https://github.com/davewalker5/FossilTracker) — fossil collection catalogue with taxonomy, provenance and geological context
- [PlateLibrary](https://github.com/davewalker5/PlateLibrary) — microscopy investigation and specimen plate library
- [FlightRecorder](https://github.com/davewalker5/FlightRecorder), [ShippingRecorder](https://github.com/davewalker5/ShippingRecorder) and [DroneFlightLog](https://github.com/davewalker5/DroneFlightLog) — personal observational logbooks

## 🐚 Computational Natural History

Increasingly, I use small computational models as a way of exploring how natural processes produce the structures and behaviour we observe.

- [LogSpiral](https://github.com/davewalker5/LogSpiral) — procedural exploration of shell growth, aperture geometry and internal architecture
- [StromatoliteGrowth](https://github.com/davewalker5/StromatoliteGrowth) — process-based models of microbial growth, sediment deposition and layered stromatolite structure
- [MolecularClockSimulation](https://github.com/davewalker5/MolecularClockSimulation) — simulation of sequence evolution and molecular-clock methods
- [ChromatophoreSimulator](https://github.com/davewalker5/ChromatophoreSimulator) — experimental graphical modelling inspired by cephalopod chromatophores
- [OdeSolver](https://github.com/davewalker5/OdeSolver) — a small environment for exploring ordinary differential equation models
- [LangtonsAnt](https://github.com/davewalker5/LangtonsAnt) — an implementation of Langton's Ant and its wonderfully surprising emergent behaviour

## 💾 Retro & Experimental Computing

Sometimes the computer itself is the thing being explored.

- [RC2014](https://github.com/davewalker5/RC2014) — programs, experiments and utilities exploring what can be made with an RC2014 Mini II Z80 computer
- [ti-84-python](https://github.com/davewalker5/ti-84-python) — Python programs and experiments for the TI-84 CE-T Python Edition
- [TelloCommander](https://github.com/davewalker5/TelloCommander) — software for controlling a Tello drone and collecting its telemetry
- [RPi-WeatherStation](https://github.com/davewalker5/RPi-WeatherStation) — Raspberry Pi weather-station experiments
